Richard “Buck” Buckman

Founder & CEO

For more than four decades, Richard “Buck” Buckman has been a trusted political strategist, government affairs professional, and relationships architect. As Founder and CEO of The Dot Connector, Buck has turned a lifetime of trusted relationships into a thriving consulting practice that helps businesses, organizations, elected officials, and entrepreneurs solve complex challenges by connecting the right people, ideas, and opportunities.

Buck’s career spans politics, government relations, economic development, media strategy, crisis management, and business consulting. He has earned the trust of leaders across government, business, and the nonprofit sector.

The philosophy behind The Dot Connector is simple but powerful: every challenge has a solution when the right people are brought together. Whether launching a company, navigating public policy, managing a crisis, expanding a business, or opening doors that once seemed impossible, Buck turns vision into action.

Throughout his career, Buck has held several influential leadership positions. As Director of Government Affairs for the nationally recognized Morelli Ratner Law Firm in New York City, he directed the firm’s Washington, D.C., operations and advised senior partners on federal and international government affairs. His work required close collaboration with Members of Congress, Senate and House leadership, executive branch officials, the White House, and leaders from both major political parties.

Earlier, Buck served as Director of Government Relations for The Eaves Law Firm, where he led congressional and government relations efforts on one of the nation’s most significant environmental health cases. Representing the people of Vieques, Puerto Rico, in their fight against the U.S. Navy’s bombing range, Buck helped generate more than twenty congressional resolutions addressing healthcare concerns and environmental justice, bringing national attention to the plight of the island’s residents.

Before entering government affairs, Buck founded Buckman & Associates, which became one of America’s most successful political consulting firms. Under his leadership, the firm compiled a record of 68 victories in 72 political campaigns, providing strategic counsel for candidates at the federal, state, and local levels, including numerous presidential campaigns. His ability to understand campaigns, messaging, coalition-building, and voter behavior established him as a respected political consultant.

What distinguishes Buck throughout his career is the relationships he has built over four decades. His network includes elected officials, corporate executives, entrepreneurs, community leaders, policy experts, media professionals, and international business leaders. Time and again, Buck has shown that meaningful relationships can accomplish what traditional consulting alone cannot.

A native of Pachuta, Mississippi, Buck studied Political Science and Economics at the University of Southern Mississippi before beginning his career on Capitol Hill working for the late Congressman Larkin Smith (R-MS). That early experience in Washington laid the foundation for a career defined by public service, strategic thinking, and leadership.

Today, Buck operates The Dot Connector from its headquarters in Diamondhead, Mississippi, with an additional office in Washington, D.C., leading a team of trusted consultants who provide strategic counsel to clients across the United States and internationally. His work includes political consulting, government affairs, business development, crisis communications, economic development, strategic partnerships, and executive advisory services.

Buckman’s career has been built on one enduring belief: “One connection is a contact. Thousands of connections, thoughtfully linked over a forty-year career, become a network capable of changing industries, shaping public policy, and moving innovation into the future.” That is The Dot Connector.

Buck is also an accomplished author and storyteller. His debut novel, Hills Over Havana, has been optioned for development as a major streaming series. Away from the office, Buck enjoys spending time with his wife, Kim, and their three beloved dogs, Hank, Branch, and Willow.

John Douglas “Doug” Adams

As Chief Financial Strategist for The Dot Connector, Adams brings a disciplined financial perspective to the firm’s strategic consulting practice. Returning to New Orleans in 2013, Adams expanded his professional experience into the insurance industry, where he continued advising clients and organizations on financial planning, risk management, and business strategy.

In 2018, Adams entered the energy sector through the commercialization of groundbreaking technology designed to mitigate hydrogen sulfide in crude oil production. This innovative solution addresses operational and safety challenges while improving workplace safety, environmental stewardship, regulatory compliance, and production efficiency.

At The Dot Connector, Adams combines his financial acumen with the firm’s relationship-driven consulting philosophy to help clients navigate today’s increasingly complex business environment. Rod McCulloch

Director of Research

Rod McCulloch is a nationally respected public opinion researcher and political strategist with more than four decades of experience in polling, survey research, and campaign consulting.

Widely recognized for his expertise in measuring voter sentiment and identifying emerging political trends, he has earned a reputation as one of the Midwest’s most accomplished pollsters. Legendary 50,000-watt radio station 890 WLS-AM has referred to him as “Illinois’ Premier Pollster,” a distinction that reflects his longstanding credibility and influence within the political community.

McCulloch was mentored by the late, internationally renowned pollster Mike McKeon, one of the nation’s most respected pioneers in political research. Early in his career, Rod served on the polling team that accurately identified and warned of the rapid rise of the Lyndon LaRouche movement during the 1980s.

Since the early 2000s, McCulloch has directed The Illinois Poll, a highly regarded semi-monthly statewide survey measuring public opinion on politics, government, and the issues shaping Illinois. Recognized for its accuracy and insightful analysis, the poll has become a trusted resource for elected officials, candidates, media organizations, business leaders, and policy professionals.

Building on that success, McCulloch has expanded his research to the national stage by creating The American Poll, which provides comprehensive public opinion research examining political, governmental, and cultural trends across the United States.

Throughout his distinguished career, Rod has played a strategic role in more than 100 successful political campaigns, providing candidates and organizations with data-driven insights that have helped shape winning strategies and informed decision-making at every level of government.

With a career spanning more than forty years, Rod McCulloch continues to be recognized as one of America’s most experienced and trusted voices in political polling.

Robert Simmons

Director of Outreach

Robert Simmons is one the most respected government relations professionals, economic development strategists, and public policy advisors, bringing more than four decades of leadership experience at the intersection of government, business, community development, and public-private partnerships. Throughout his distinguished career, Simmons has successfully guided major commercial, industrial, residential, infrastructure, and governmental initiatives that have generated hundreds of millions of dollars in investment while helping shape the economic landscape of the Mississippi Gulf Coast and communities throughout the state.

Since 1993, Robert Simmons has provided strategic consulting services in government relations, legislative affairs, economic development, regulatory strategy, and public-private partnerships for clients at the local, state, and federal levels. His extensive portfolio has included private casino developments, hospitality projects, commercial shopping centers, local governments, the Gulfport-Biloxi International Airport, Mississippi Power Company, The GEO Group, Keefe Group, Sentinel, Harrison County, and numerous public and private organizations seeking experienced guidance in navigating government processes and advancing complex economic development initiatives.

Before entering private consulting, Simmons held several of the most influential economic development leadership positions in Mississippi. As Director of Community Development for the City of Biloxi, he played a pivotal role in planning initiatives that ultimately helped pave the way for the transformation of the Mississippi Gulf Coast through the development of the casino gaming industry. His leadership contributed to the creation of Biloxi’s Waterfront Master Plan, one of the most significant economic development initiatives in the region’s modern history.

Simmons later served as Director of Planning, Community Development, and Recreation for the City of Hattiesburg, where he managed four major municipal divisions, supervised a staff of more than sixty professionals, and directed a wide range of planning, transportation, recreation, housing, and economic development programs. Under his leadership, Hattiesburg’s Neighborhood Improvement Program earned national recognition by receiving the U.S. Conference of Mayors City Livability Award, while the city was also selected as a finalist for the prestigious All-America City Award presented by the National Civic League.

As Director of Economic Development for the City of Pass Christian, Simmons successfully recruited and coordinated one of the city’s first major commercial retail developments in many years, further demonstrating his ability to attract private investment and foster sustainable economic growth. Earlier in his career, while serving as Project Planner for the Southern Mississippi Planning and Development District, he prepared Community Development Block Grant applications, Urban Development Action Grant proposals, regional planning studies, and economic development strategies that benefited communities throughout South Mississippi.

Over the course of his career, Simmons has been directly involved in economic and community development initiatives responsible for generating well over $300 million in public and private investment. His expertise encompasses government affairs, legislative strategy, economic development, municipal planning, redevelopment, development finance, public policy, budgeting, organizational leadership, and the successful execution of large-scale public-private partnerships.

His educational background reflects an equally strong commitment to professional excellence. Simmons earned a Master of Urban and Regional Planning from the University of Mississippi after receiving his Bachelor of Arts in Political Science, with a minor in Economics, from Alcorn State University. He further strengthened his expertise through advanced certifications in Development Finance from the University of San Francisco and the New York University School of Real Estate, as well as certification as a Community Development Specialist.

Throughout his distinguished career, Simmons has been recognized as one of Mississippi’s leading voices in economic development and public service. His numerous honors include induction into the Alcorn State University Hall of Honor, the Distinguished Alumni Award from the National Association for Equal Opportunity in Higher Education, appointment to the Alcorn State University Foundation Board, and selection as one of Mississippi’s 50 Outstanding Leaders. Governor Ray Mabus appointed Simmons to the Mississippi Department of Economic Development Advisory Council, where he helped shape statewide economic development policy during a period of significant growth.

Beyond his professional accomplishments, Simmons has remained deeply committed to civic leadership and community service. He has served on numerous governmental, educational, charitable, and nonprofit boards, including the Seafood Industry Museum, the Greater Biloxi Economic Development Foundation, the Mississippi Rivers and Harbors Commission, the Jackson State University College of Public Service Council of Advisors, and as Chairman of the Trustee Board of Tabernacle Missionary Baptist Church in D’Iberville, Mississippi.

Today, Robert Simmons continues to be recognized as one of the Gulf South’s foremost authorities on government relations, legislative affairs, economic development, and strategic public-private partnerships. His unmatched understanding of government processes, combined with decades of trusted relationships across Mississippi and Washington, D.C., enables him to successfully connect public policy with private-sector investment, creating opportunities that strengthen communities, expand businesses, and drive long-term economic growth.
