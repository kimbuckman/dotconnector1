Political

Messaging, strategy, and campaign insight for leaders who need practical direction.

Our CEO and Founder, Richard “Buck” Buckman, began his political career over 40 years ago after interning with US Congressman Larken Smith of Mississippi in Washington, D.C. Smith later died in a plane crash, an event that deeply affected Buck and shaped his future challenges.

He later opened his own offices on the Mississippi Gulf Coast and went on to direct or participate in nearly 100 political campaigns, including municipal, statewide, US legislative, and Presidential races across the United States. As director, he has maintained a 96.4% win ratio. Buck brings deep experience across the political spectrum and its nuances, along with field-tested campaign strategies that work. The Dot Connector has also added qualified team members with unique political experience.

That experience drives The Dot Connector's success. Without it, the firm would be just another consulting shingle, limited by its experience, education, and a vast array of key field contacts. If you’re planning to enter the political arena, schedule a consultation with Buck and his team today to discuss your next move and get started.
