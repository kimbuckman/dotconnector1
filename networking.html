Networking

Intentional relationship building that connects the right people, ideas, and institutions.

Networking has always mattered, but the internet and personal computers made it essential. It opens doors to opportunities that might otherwise be missed. If nurtured, these relationships can become valuable assets down the road.

Networking shows competence, credibility, clarity, and connectivity—the four C’s. It demonstrates that your company or organization is skilled and capable. It establishes trust, warmth, and ethical standards. It helps communicate what you need and how others can help. It also shows the quality and breadth of your network. If you’re not really a networker, you should be, and you can start now. Start online, attend meetings and events, and connect with industry peers at related conferences. As your circle of contacts grows, your knowledge base expands, creating new opportunities to help meet your goals.

At The Dot Connector, we’re always glad to meet new and interesting people, smile and shake hands, listen to their stories, and get to know them. Remember the old adage? “Sometimes it’s not what you know, but who you know.” We absolutely thrive on investing our time in other people, building friendships and business relationships that create clear value for our clients and for us. Networking is at the core of our business. Our success proves it.
